# TrainerTrade — Terms of Service (draft)

**This is a starting draft, not legal advice, and hasn't been reviewed by a
solicitor.** It's written to be genuinely usable — clear, and covering the
right ground — but before this app has real users, get an actual solicitor
to look it over once. UK-focused (governing law, wording) since that's the
target market.

## 1. What this is

TrainerTrade helps Pokémon TCG collectors nearby find each other, see who
has cards they want and wants cards they have, and message each other to
arrange a trade. That's it. TrainerTrade is **not**:

- A marketplace — no cards are bought or sold through the app.
- An escrow or payment service — the app never holds money, cards, or
  anything of value, and never will as part of arranging a trade.
- A verification service — TrainerTrade does not verify that any card is
  genuine, that any user's identity is exactly who they claim, or that
  either side of a trade did what they said they would.

TrainerTrade is a tool to help you find and talk to someone. What happens
after that — whether to trade, what to trade, where to meet, whether to
ship something — is a decision made entirely by the two users involved,
not by TrainerTrade.

## 2. Trades are between users, not with TrainerTrade

Every trade arranged through the app is a private agreement between the
users involved. TrainerTrade is not a party to it, does not guarantee it
will happen, does not guarantee the condition or authenticity of any card
involved, and is not responsible if:

- a card turns out to be fake, damaged, or not as described,
- the other person doesn't show up, doesn't ship, or doesn't follow
  through on what was agreed,
- you're unhappy with a trade after it's complete.

This is the same basis on which Facebook Marketplace, Gumtree, and similar
platforms operate: they connect people, the exchange itself is between the
people involved.

## 3. Meeting up and shipping are at your own risk

If you choose to meet someone in person, you're responsible for your own
safety in doing so. See the in-app "Meeting up safely" guide for
suggestions — but following that guidance, or not, is your decision, and
TrainerTrade isn't responsible for what happens at a meetup.

If you choose to send a card by post to complete a trade (where that
feature is available), that's entirely at your own risk. TrainerTrade does
not insure, guarantee, or take any responsibility for items lost, damaged,
or not sent in return. Think carefully before shipping first to someone
without an established trade history.

## 4. Your account

- You must provide accurate information when registering, including a
  postcode that reflects roughly where you actually are (used only to
  find nearby matches — never shown precisely to other users).
- You're responsible for anything that happens on your account.
- You must be 18 or over to use TrainerTrade. _(Flagging this explicitly:
  this is a real product decision, not boilerplate — set deliberately
  because minors coordinating in-person meetups with strangers through an
  app raises safety and liability questions this document doesn't
  currently try to solve. Revisit only with a proper plan for it: age
  verification, parental consent, meetup safeguards.)_
- We can suspend or remove an account that violates these terms, receives
  credible reports, or otherwise poses a risk to other users — without
  that being an admission that TrainerTrade is responsible for resolving
  the underlying dispute.

## 5. Reporting and moderation

The in-app report feature lets you flag another user — for a suspected
fake card, a no-show, harassment, or anything else that concerns you.
Reports are reviewed, but TrainerTrade isn't obligated to resolve disputes
between users, issue refunds (none are ever held, so there's nothing to
refund), or act as an arbitrator. Its role is limited to account-level
action (warnings, suspension, removal) where warranted.

## 6. No liability for what TrainerTrade doesn't control

To the fullest extent the law allows, TrainerTrade (and whoever operates
it) isn't liable for any loss, damage, dispute, or injury arising from:

- a trade, meetup, or shipment arranged through the app,
- the authenticity, condition, or value of any card,
- the conduct of any user, on or off the app,
- inaccurate card data or pricing pulled from third-party sources.

These terms may be updated as the app changes. Continued use after an
update means you accept the current version.
